Improved proposal & real-camera implementation scope

Problem: Manual shelf rounds leave availability gaps and misplaced products unnoticed. Alerts need ownership and evidence that the issue was actually resolved.

Solution: Detect visible shelf shortages and incorrect SKU placement, create one actionable task per zone and issue, and close only after three clear, healthy samples. Staff acknowledgement alone never resolves a task. Unavailable backroom stock is recorded as a reason, while the issue stays open.

Reliability: Blocked views remain UNKNOWN and reset confirmation streaks. A 20-second obstruction creates a camera-health task. Open shelf tasks get a reminder after 60 seconds in this demo. Offline cameras cannot verify recovery. Uncertain SKU predictions in the real integration must also remain UNKNOWN.

Real-camera MVP: One fixed camera, six to eight visually distinct SKUs, labelled training images, held-out capture-session testing, YOLO detection, configured shelf zones and a backend incident store. This browser prototype uses simulated observations and local storage; it does not provide trained detection, shared staff accounts or phone alerts.

Validation targets, not results: ≥90% event precision/recall, visible-count MAE ≤1 unit/zone, p95 alert latency ≤10 seconds, and ≤1 false alert/camera-hour. Test at least 60 staged events and an hour of normal/obstructed footage. Count visible units only, never hidden inventory.
